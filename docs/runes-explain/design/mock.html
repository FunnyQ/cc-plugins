Why a fork, not Jev, decides what to explain

The fork already read the whole session, so it picks the topic itself without sending anything off your machine.

Photocopying your whole notebook and handing the copy to a friend, instead of tearing out one page and posting it to a stranger.

The three parts

A
TranscriptEverything said and done in this session, in order.
B
ForkA copy of the conversation asked one extra question, then thrown away.
C
JevA small outside model that scores only the snippet we send it.

What happens when you press

  1. The mod copies the conversation (A) into a fork (B). Nothing is added to your chat.
  2. The fork looks at your last exchange and the whole session, and picks what is worth explaining.
  3. It answers in the side pane, or writes a page like this one when a picture helps.

Jev (C) would only ever see the snippet we chose, and that snippet would leave your machine.

The fork is the one reader that was in the room the whole time.

What isPlaced: false means

The pane exists, but your terminal is too narrow to show it, so it waits until there is room.

A table booked at a full restaurant: the booking is real, you sit down when a table frees up.

Why it happens

A pane you did not ask for only takes a seat from 144 columns. Pressing the button may or may not count as asking; we have not measured it yet.

When the pane is not placed, the mod shows a short toast so you know the answer is waiting.

3 · Long session recap: timeline, before/after, then a spread

cc-plugins · session recap25 Oct 2026 · 17:48

How the explain button got planned

We turned "explain this to me" into a button that forks the session, and a hidden helper checks the drawings before you see them.

What happened, in order

  1. 09:40The ideaSingle-page HTML explainers were being asked for by hand, again and again.
  2. 10:15Where it livesNot in cockpit: a page running inside cockpit could reach its private inbox. It became a rune instead.
  3. 11:02Who decides the topicJev was dropped; the session's own fork already knows everything.
  4. 13:30A test before the planA throwaway mod proved a hidden helper may run Chrome and look at a screenshot.
  5. 16:20The lookThree rolls of the dice, then cold-press paper and ink-and-wash drawings.

Before and after

Before

You typed "explain this as an HTML page with diagrams", waited, and opened the file yourself.

After

You press one button. The answer lands in the pane, or a checked page opens on its own.

The page's journey

A
WrittenThe fork writes the drawing and the words into this paper template.
B
CheckedA hidden helper photographs the page at two widths and fixes labels that overlap.
C
OpenedYour browser opens it, even when the check could not run.

One press, one fork, and nothing new in your chat.
